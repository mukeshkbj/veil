- **Design 5:** The hero is a line chart with a shaded band, and a chart in a 2-minute video reads as a dashboard, not a product.
- **Impact 7:** "Is my serum working?" is the track's own moment, and a product that tells you to buy less is credible.
- **Idea 7:** Treating the vendor score as a noisy instrument is smart, but five other entries in this pile pitch the same "honest verdict".

## Cross-cutting observations

1. **Sameness risk.** Nine of the fifteen ideas pitch "we measure the noise and refuse to give credit we cannot prove" (Trial Bench, Trigger Lab, Week-6, Receipts, Week 3, Other Cheek, Fade, Headroom in part, Lopsided's lighting gate). In a pool of 1,125, a judge sees that sentence many times. The entry needs a *picture* that is its own, not just the honesty argument.
2. **Two ideas have the strongest picture** (Lopsided's split face and Unanimous's four faces). Two have the strongest rigour (Other Cheek and Trial Bench). Nothing in the set has both.
3. **Agentic bonus.** Only Trial Bench, Look Compiler, Minimum Dose and Barely There have a real loop. Lopsided and Other Cheek need a small agent layer to collect that bonus.
4. **Circularity trap.** Simulation output fed back into analysis (Receipts, Trial Bench calibration, Other Cheek controls) can read as vendor-grading-vendor. Other Cheek escapes it by framing the simulation as an instrument test ("did the pipeline detect a planted change?") and never as efficacy evidence.
5. **Track fit.** Unanimous, Second Shade and Look Compiler fit the eCommerce VTO track wording. Everything else is Skin AI. No idea fully bridges both, and the sponsor invites combining.

## The longitudinal demo problem

Ideas affected: Trial Bench, Trigger Lab, Week-6 Wall, Receipts, Week 3, Fade, and partly The Other Cheek. Their value appears over weeks, and a judge gives the video about 60 seconds. A fake time-lapse is the most tempting fix and the most damaging, because a skeptical judge reads a synthetic series as proof of nothing.

How I would make a 2-minute video compelling *and* honest:

1. **Put the instant value first.** Whatever the product shows from a single photo (a baseline, a noise floor, an asymmetry map) must be on screen in the first 15 seconds. Time is the second act, not the opening.
2. **Use what is measurable on day 0.** Two same-day selfies give a noise floor immediately. A planted simulated change gives a sensitivity check immediately. Both can be shown live, in seconds, with no waiting. This is the single best trick in the set, and it belongs to The Other Cheek and Trial Bench.
3. **Capture real time with the days left.** Today is Oct 21 and the deadline is Nov 2. A real 10-day trial on two or three teammates gives day 0 / 3 / 7 / 10 captures with real timestamps. Show the capture dates on screen. Ten real days beats six imagined weeks.
4. **Label compression and fixtures on screen, permanently.** A persistent chip reading "REAL CAPTURES, Oct 22 to Nov 1, 3 people" or "REPLAY DATA, schema-faithful" costs nothing and buys trust. Judges forgive a labelled fixture and punish a hidden one.
5. **Keep the time scrub to about 6 seconds.** One gesture, one counter, one stamp. Do not narrate a chart.
6. **Make the null result a hero, not an apology.** "Pores: cannot tell, change is inside the noise. Don't repurchase on this evidence." is the most memorable line in the set because nothing else on the table says it.
7. **Claim the instrument, not the efficacy.** Say "we can detect a planted 9-point change and we reject a planted null". Never say "this serum works". A derm on the panel and a technical judge both relax at that sentence.
8. **Offer a live path a judge can verify in 30 seconds.** A "scan yourself" button (one selfie, instant result) plus a "replay a 10-day trial" toggle covers both people who run it and people who only watch.

## Single most likely winner

**Lopsided**, as-is. A judge grasps it in one sentence, the split-face frame is the one thing in the pile they will remember, and it needs no weeks of data. Its weak spot is Impact (episodic use, thin sleep-side evidence), which is exactly what the hybrid fixes.

## Proposed hybrid: "Control Face"

*Tagline: your face is its own control group.*

Lopsided supplies the picture and the instant hook. The Other Cheek supplies the rigour and turns an interesting curiosity into a decision tool. A thin agent layer from Trial Bench collects the sponsor's bonus. Second Shade and Receipts supply the money card.

| Act | What the user sees | Borrowed from | Why it beats every single idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Reveal (first photo) | Midline hairline, one half greys out, masks bloom, ratio badge "Redness: left 2.6x" | Lopsided | Instant value, the memorable image |
| 2. Choose a side | "Your left cheek is the worse side. Put the serum there only. Your right is the control." | Other Cheek | Turns asymmetry from a trivia into a design: the asymmetry ratio *is* the measurement |
| 3. Prove the instrument | Planted known change recovered, planted null rejected, noise floor from two same-day shots | Other Cheek, Trial Bench | Honest, instant, and verifiable without waiting weeks |
| 4. Real time, compressed | 10 real days, labelled, with the lighting-imbalance score per scan so a bad scan is rejected on screen | Lopsided gate, Trial Bench | Solves the longitudinal problem with labelled real data |
| 5. Agent decides | Agent chooses when to stop early or buy one extra shot for the least certain concern, within a unit budget | Trial Bench | Real perceive-decide-act-verify loop for the agentic bonus |
| 6. Verdict and money | "Redness: real effect +9 (noise ±3). Pores: cannot tell. Skip the refill, saves $312/yr" with a Keep / Skip action and a "better aimed at your top gap" link | Receipts, Headroom | Retail-credible impact, a call to action, and a bridge toward the VTO track |

Why it wins: the split face solves Lopsided's two biggest weaknesses (fake asymmetry from lighting, and "so what?") and solves The Other Cheek's weakest point (a rigorous tool with no striking visual). It keeps the instant first-photo value, so the video never depends on weeks of data.

Cheapest honest build order: (1) one live scan to confirm mask format, (2) midline split and ratio math, (3) planted positive and negative controls, (4) lighting gate, (5) replay mode, (6) the 10-day real capture, (7) verdict screen, (8) agent stop rule.

## What I would punish in this pile

- Any entry whose 20-second demo is a chart (Trial Bench, Trigger Lab, Week-6 Wall, Receipts).
- Any entry that needs a synthetic six-week dataset to look finished and does not say so.
- Already Own It: the API is replaceable by a hex formula.
- Overclaiming: "mMASI score" (Fade) and "purge versus reaction" (Week 3, Week-6) without validation, and "this is why you wake up lopsided" (Lopsided) with mixed evidence. Use "estimate", "pattern consistent with" and "hypothesis".
