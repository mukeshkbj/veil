# Round 2 judging brief

Read research/brief.md (criteria, rules, unit budget). Candidates are the 15 ideas in research/round2/{returns-economist,inclusion,brand-buyer,daily-habit,contrarian}.md plus one incumbent, "Other Cheek" (research/decision.md: a 4-week split-face skincare trial with a personal noise band and a planted-effect self-test; already built, replay demo only, no live API run yet).

Rules:
- IGNORE the builders' own scores and critic sections. Score independently.
- Judge as if you saw only a 2-minute video, 3 screenshots and a description, built to a high standard by one builder in about 10 days with 1,000 API units.
- Score each of the 16 candidates 1-10 on Technological Implementation, Design, Potential Impact, Quality of Idea, with one sentence each. Be harsh: 9+ means "would plausibly win first place on that criterion out of ~150 submissions".
- Then name your top 3, and for the top 1 say exactly what would raise each criterion to 9+ (changes, merges with another idea, or a sharper framing). Merges across ideas are encouraged.
- Flag any idea that many other entrants will likely also build.

Write your file to research/round2/judge-<your-name>.md. Do not edit other files. Plain prose, no em dashes.
