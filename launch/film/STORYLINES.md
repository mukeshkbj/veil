# Veil launch film: three storylines

Length 36 s. Audience: a hackathon judge who has seen 200 entries today. They must lean in by 3 s and feel something by the end. Research: `C:\Users\mukes\honey\veil-launch-refs\analysis\`.

## A. The other cheek (recommended)

A dark cheek fills the frame. A finger rubs white cream into it, and the cream stays. The words on the tube say "Invisible on all skin." Three more faces in mirrors, each with the same grey film. One in four skin-of-colour patients name this as a reason they struggle with sunscreen. One question on a white field: "Will anyone see this on me?" Veil answers it on her face: a line slides across, the bare cheek on one side and the swiped cheek on the other, and the number counts to 8.4. Ghost. Then the shelf re-ranks for her skin, and the one at the top vanishes on her. A run of faces in sunlight follows, each tagged with its own skin colour, faster and faster, until one woman closes her eyes in the sun. The film ends: "Find the sunscreen that disappears on you."

Why it works: the Real Tone shape (failure first, then release), with the product as the turn, not a coda. The judge sees the cost in frame 1, the mechanism in the middle and a feeling at the end. The cast is brown and Black skin from the first frame, which no film in the research set did with a product mechanism.

## B. Two cheeks

One face, held for the whole film. The left cheek is bare and the right cheek is swiped. The camera never cuts. Type and the Veil interface build around her: the midline, the tiles, the number, the shelf, the try-on, which changes her right cheek product by product until it matches the left. The film ends when the two cheeks are the same.

Why not: one shot is elegant, but 36 s on one still portrait has no tension curve, and stock footage gives no 36 s locked-off face.

## C. The swatch

No people at first. Swatches of skin colour sweep across a white field like paint chips, and a white streak passes over each one. On the dark chips the streak stays visible; on the light chips it disappears. Then the chips become faces.

Why not: an abstraction about skin repeats the Black Girl Sunscreen failure (a film about skin with no skin in it). It is craft without a person.

## Pick

A. The user asked for no stops, so the recommended storyline goes to SPEC.md.
